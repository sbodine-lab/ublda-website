# Michigan consulting club homepage review

Reviewed September 21, 2026 after Sam requested a shared homepage that accounts for prospective members and businesses without splitting them into separate paths at entry.

| Club | Observed homepage strategy |
| --- | --- |
| [MECC](https://www.meccconsulting.com/) | Introduces the student consulting organization, then projects and community together. Recruiting and projects are navigation links; audience labels appear in the footer. |
| [BOND](https://www.bond-consulting.org/) | Connects local client work with member development in its welcome and mission, followed by community, testimonials and projects. |
| [APEX](https://apexconsulting.org/) | Shared welcome combines client engagements and member experience; dedicated organization/student calls to action occur farther down. |
| [180 Degrees Michigan](https://www.180dcmichigan.com/) | Starts with identity, who they are and what they do; analyst and client sections follow. |
| [Atlas](https://www.atlasconsultinggroup.org/) | Shared introduction followed by project experience, professional development and community; audience-specific navigation in the footer. |
| [Nexecon](https://www.nexeconconsulting.com/) | Combines client work and member experience in the introduction, then people, projects and professional development. |
| [Capital Consulting Group](https://www.ccgumich.org/) | Introduces club identity and client purpose alongside member learning, then work areas and team. |
| [Michigan Data Consulting](https://www.michigandataconsulting.org/) | Uses project experience, skill development and community to tell its story; work and recruiting remain navigation destinations. |
| [TAMID Michigan](https://www.michigantamid.org/) | Leads with club identity and activity pillars, with Apply and Contact in navigation. |
| [NOBE Michigan](https://www.nobeumich.org/) | Search-indexed homepage describes mission, consulting work and members together; direct page fetch timed out. |

MEG was identified through its official club profile, but its current homepage could not be retrieved and did not inform the design. This is a broad peer review, not a claim to cover every Michigan organization.

## Applied to UBLDA

The recurring pattern is an integrated club story with ordinary navigation, not an audience gate. UBLDA’s homepage now follows: shared introduction → first client → project process → club community → potential project areas → project managers → external research. One hero link leads to the first client. Apply stays in the header and closing section, with a project-inquiry link beside the service areas.

Copy connects student learning with the client’s business question in the same paragraph. Research remains neutral under “Accessibility in business.” Preserve the Fall 2026 launch, first-client status, future engagement dates, and actual two-part scope. Do not borrow peers’ track records, project counts, testimonials, professional-development programs, or claims of results.
